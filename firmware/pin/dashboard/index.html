Switch to

The pin's side button switches to the next member marked Quick Switch, most PluralKit messages first (or to anyone, if nobody is marked). Press it twice for nobody fronting, three times (or hold it) for the clock.

Recent switches

The pin and PluralKit each keep a front history. Switches made on the pin (side button, dashboard) are only on the pin until you sync; switches PluralKit made while the pin was off can be missing on the pin.

Time fronting

Battery

The pin notes its battery voltage once a minute and keeps the last 4 days, even if the battery runs flat. To measure a full charge: charge it fully, clear the log, then use the pin on battery until it switches off.

The pin tries these networks in this order and joins the first one in range (untick "Use this network" to have it ignore one for a while - e.g. home Wi-Fi, to test your hotspot). If the pin ends up on no network at all, turn Wi-Fi off and on again on the pin's Settings screen: that switches every network back on. A phone's hotspot works too: on an iPhone, turn on Settings → Personal Hotspot → Maximize Compatibility (the pin only does 2.4 GHz) and keep that screen open until the pin has joined.

Sync with PluralKit

When you switch in PluralKit (e.g. pk;switch in Discord), the pin picks it up within a minute and adds it to its front history. The pin only reads from PluralKit, never changes anything there.

Enter your system ID if your front is public, or your token (send pk;token to PluralKit in a DM) to also work with a private front. The token stays on the pin and is never shown here again.

Instant, through your relay

With the relay running on an always-on computer (see relay/README.md), PluralKit tells the relay about each switch and the pin gets it straight away. Checking PluralKit directly (above) then only happens if the relay can't be reached.

In Discord, send pk;export to PluralKit, save the file it sends you, then pick it here.

Photos from PluralKit

Gives every member their PluralKit avatar as their photo, for the image modes. Photos go on the pin's SD card; members marked "Fast photo" in the member editor keep theirs in the pin's own memory instead, so they show straight away.
